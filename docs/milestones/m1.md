## Findings during M1

- **Engine bug caught by the property harness:** house equity ignored the house cost basis when the house was flat in lots (users long and short at different prices). Invariant 7 fired; fixed with a regression test (`fix(engine): count house cost basis…`).
- **Accessibility:** language switcher violated WCAG 2.5.3 (label in name); the `account:<id>` WebSocket channel accepted foreign ids (mapped to self, no leak) — now refused.
- **Simplifications to review:** liquidation is full-only at mark (partial-first in M4); house quote = FV ∓ half-spread without size impact (ADR-0003 full model in M4).
