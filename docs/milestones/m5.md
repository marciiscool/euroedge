## Founder to confirm (review gate: math)

- **Balance root = settled cash** of wallet accounts with a positive balance, not equity (no unrealized PnL), snapshotted at the first engine event of each hour.
- **Signer trusts Valkey:** it signs what the engine published there. The Vault's daily limit bounds the damage if Valkey is compromised; checking each reservation against the Postgres event log is a possible hardening step.

- **Claim caps:** 10 000 tUSDC per 24 h, 100 000 lifetime per wallet.
- **Vault daily withdrawal limit:** 1 000 000 tUSDC across all users per UTC day. It bounds a leaked signer key.
- **Withdrawal deadlines.**
  - The engine uses ms; the signer must convert them to seconds.
  - The engine's 1-hour TTL (M4-18) must match what the signer puts on-chain.
- **Smart wallets.**
  - Deployed smart wallets use the permit path via ERC-1271.
  - Counterfactual (EIP-6492) wallets go through the approve + deposit batch (M5-15), not through permit.
- **Relayer limits:** 5 relays per wallet and 20 per IP per hour.
- **Base Sepolia deploy:** needs your Safe address, a funded deployer key and a Basescan API key (runbook in `docs/runbooks/contracts-deploy.md`).
