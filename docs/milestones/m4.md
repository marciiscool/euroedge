## Founder to confirm

- **All quote, flow-shift and house-risk values in ADR-0019** (a, s, invCap, γ, x_ref, α, κ, τ, wallet cap, invHardCap, daily loss limit).

- `fundingOiCapUsdc` per window (A 2 M, B 1 M, C 0.5 M USDC): the net skew at which funding reaches its cap.
- `partialLiquidationBufferBps: 2500`: partial liquidation restores equity to 125 % of maintenance margin.
- **CB5 is not defined in the repo.** PLAN R3 only says "house balance must stay ≥ 0 (CB5 + top-up runbook)". Please give the trigger, effect and release from the brief.
- CB7 uses house equity = cash + uPnL. ADR-0013 also counts accrued, unsettled funding. Funding settles on the hour without an intra-hour accrual, so that term is 0 here.
- Stops trigger on the **mark**, not the quote, and a fired stop pays the taker fee. A stop that would fire at once is rejected rather than executed.
- Resting orders survive circuit breakers: opening orders wait, reducing ones still fill.
- Dividends (M4-16):
  - **Withholding 15 % on longs** (proposal; per-market config to be set).
  - Shorts pay gross, and the withheld part stays with the house.
  - Cash moves at the **local primary ex-date** (00:00 exchange time).
  - Not yet sent automatically: a service must emit `DividendExDate` from the dividend calendar (next step, with corporate actions M4-17).
- Withdrawals (M4-18):
  - **TTL 1 hour** (proposal): the EIP-712 deadline.
  - Only free collateral can leave: equity − initial margin at the current window caps, capped at cash. A position in a reduce-only window or a market without data locks withdrawals until that clears.
  - Only wallet accounts can withdraw; dev accounts cannot.
  - A rejected request is re-evaluated if retried; only reservations are idempotent.
- Limit orders fill in full only (no partial fills), against the house quote, at the then-current price (price improvement goes to the user).
