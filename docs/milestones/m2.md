## Founder to check

2. **Xetra year-end.** The brief says Xetra closes at 14:00 on 24 and 31 Dec. The cross-check says Xetra is **closed** on 24 and 31 Dec, with a 14:00 close on the last trading day (30 Dec). The calendar follows the cross-check.
3. **XCSE close.** The calendar has continuous trading 09:00–16:55 plus a closing auction to 17:00. ADR-0017 says "continuous 09:00–17:00".
4. **Basket constituents.** Constituents, weights, the EE-Semis 35 % cap and the start level of 1,000 are drafts.
5. **Market data to confirm.** ADR ratios for TTE, LVMUY and STM, and the Tradegate symbols (live adapters map by ISIN).

## Deliberate gaps

- **Parquet input.** Not implemented; the CSV format is the interchange.
- **Pyth wire format.** Assumed from the Pyth Pro docs shape. Only a fixture tests it until the founder's key arrives.
- **Replay tier.** Streams ticks at their recorded timestamps, while downstream services still use the wall clock. A shared virtual clock comes with the M3 backtest CLI.
- **Tick retention.** Not enabled, because live recordings must be kept for M3 parameter fitting. Compression is on.
- **Currency normalisation.** Ticks keep their source currency. GBX/100 and the DKK/GBP conversion happen in fv-core (M3); that is also when NOVO and SHEL get enabled.
