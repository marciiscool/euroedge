## Founder decisions needed

1. **Band semantics in Window A** (`docs/backtest/README.md`).
   - On simulated data, bands cover the next open only about 73 % of the time; the target is 98–99.5 %.
   - The fit proposes σ floors of B 79 bp, C 84 bp and D 78 bp. Window A would need 98 bp (±2.5 %), which would disable the band clamp while the exchange is open.
   - Proposal: apply the B/C/D floors, and score Window A against the close instead.
2. **CB3 interpretation.** CB3 only fires while a live source is scheduled. Otherwise every market would be "degraded" every night and weekend, which contradicts 24/7 trading.
3. **Breaker effects in the engine.** Every breaker blocks new exposure, and "degraded" doubles the house half-spread. Please confirm the 2x factor.
4. **Basket parameters.** The basket constituents, the EE-Semis 35 % cap and the 50 % coverage rule are drafts.
